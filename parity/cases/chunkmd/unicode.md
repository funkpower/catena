# Ἀθῆναι and Μόσχα

Le café était naïve, and the ﬁrst ligature in that sentence is a single
codepoint rather than two letters, which is the sort of thing that moves a
token boundary without moving a character boundary.

東京から来た手紙には、日付が二つ書いてあった。One was the date it was written
and the other was the date it was meant to be read, and the difference between
them is the whole subject of this section.

مرحبا — the paragraph above is followed by a right-to-left greeting, then by a
combining acute on a bare e: é. None of this is unusual in a converted
book, and all of it has broken a chunker at some point.

Straße, Ægypt, ﬁrst, ॐ. The list is arbitrary. Its purpose is to be awkward.
